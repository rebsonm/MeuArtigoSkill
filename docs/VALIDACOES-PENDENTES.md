# What Meu Artigo has not yet been proven to do

Meu Artigo is an **experimental public beta**. Passing software checks does not mean that a study produced with it is scientifically correct, that all references are reliable or that it works equally well for all disciplines and platforms.

## Current evidence and limits

| Area | What remains unverified |
| --- | --- |
| Complete article workflow | An independently reviewed real research project, from initial question to final manuscript |
| Quality of evidence and citations | Consistent expert assessment that statements accurately reflect the actual sources |
| Practical usefulness | Measured performance and user experience with researchers from different fields and methodological approaches |
| Platform operation | Fully observed installation, continuation and authorized storage across ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini |
| Google Drive and databases | Successful operations depend on each researcher's real permissions and connections |
| C.A.D.A. outcomes | Evidence that task tracking causes better research outcomes or measurable productivity gains |
| Language support | Observed quality of conversations in different languages and of manuscript-specific output |

**Evaluation status: PENDING.** These items should not be presented as established scientific results.

## How this affects you

Use Meu Artigo to organize, explore and scrutinize your research, but verify original sources, investigate contrary evidence, make independent methodological decisions and review every article before submission. A working ZIP and a completed technical test cannot guarantee academic acceptance, ethics compliance or an original contribution.

The source repository may contain detailed engineering reports. They are technical documentation, not proof that scientific or user-centered evaluations are complete.
