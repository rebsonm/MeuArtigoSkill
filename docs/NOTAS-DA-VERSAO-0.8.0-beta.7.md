# My Article — beta version 0.8.0-beta.7

This update deepens the methodological guidance for **quantitative and mixed methods research**, preserving qualitative traditions and the researcher's scientific responsibility.

<a id="o-que-foi-ampliado"></a>
## What was expanded
- Critical foundation of the **Research Onion** (*Research Onion*) by Saunders, Lewis and Thornhill, with six decision layers and warning that they are not deterministic.
- Differentiation between quantitative purposes **descriptive, associative, predictive and causal**, with appropriate criteria for unit of analysis, measurement, sampling, data processing, inference and limitations.
- Distinction between **mono-method, multi-method and mixed methods**; mixed convergent, explanatory and exploratory sequential designs.
- Integration of qualitative and quantitative components by **connecting, building, merging and embedding**, according to Fetters, Curry and Creswell (2013), with discussion on discordant results and *joint displays*.
- Bibliography reinforced with methodological sources and scientific reporting guidelines, distinguishing what was consulted in text from what was verified by metadata.
- Updated Skill instructions and design guide to present relevant alternatives without imposing extensive questionnaires or creating new record families.

<a id="limites"></a>
## Limits
The Research Onion is presented in its own words, without reproduction of Saunders' protected image. **Methodological guidance and approval of technical tests do not equate to an empirical assessment of the scientific quality of the Skill.** No new research, statistical analyses, interviews or evaluations with participants were performed in this update.
