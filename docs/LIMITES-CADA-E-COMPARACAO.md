# Scope of C.A.D.A. and independent evaluation protocol

<a id="o-que-o-cada-faz-e-o-que-não-faz"></a>
## What does C.A.D.A. Do's and don'ts

**C.A.D.A. = Capture, Assign, Set deadline and Monitor** documented administrative demands. At Skill My Article it is the layer of **operational governance of execution**, not a new method of literature review and not a demonstration that the research will be more scientifically correct.

| Layer | Mechanisms | Appropriate evidence | What does NOT prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| C.A.D.A., 11_CADA_Control and 15_CADA_Dashboard | Responsible, deadline, dependency, next step, status, documented discharge | Task Records and Completion Artifacts | Scientific quality, search coverage, effective human review |
| Scientific procedure | Protocol, searches, deduplication, screening, full text, analysis and synthesis | Search_ID, Record_ID, Evidence_ID, Claim_ID and original documents | Causal effect of C.A.D.A. |
| Scientific decision and validation | DEC_ID, GATE_ID, methodological judgments and human review | Original manifestations and traceable decision record | Quality automatically certified due to a gate occurring |
| Execution and provenance | TRACE, receipts and snapshots | Effectively captured hashes and results | That an academic statement or conclusion is true |

Management objects may relate to a scientific record, but the link does NOT transfer their epistemological authority. A **DONE** task does not promote sources to verified, does not approve the protocol, and does not replace a GATE-0004/GATE-0006.

<a id="auditabilidade-técnica-agora"></a>
## Technical auditability now

The `scripts/audit_governance_boundary.py` script does not depend on paid applications. With Python 3.10+:

```bash
python scripts/audit_governance_boundary.py audit /projeto --strict
python scripts/audit_governance_boundary.py audit /projeto --strict --output 00_Gestao_e_Continuidade/GOVERNANCE_BOUNDARY_REPORT.json
python scripts/audit_governance_boundary.py check-plan benchmarks/cada_comparison_protocol_v1.json
```

The report separates **management activities**, **scientific records** and **technical evidence**. It identifies tasks marked DONE with empty or tautological operational evidence and gates with just “DONE”/“CADA-0001” as supposed scientific proof. Verification of scientific origin/quality remains in specialized mechanisms for verifying sources, evidence, records and human review; This control does NOT replace them.

The module returns `scientific_quality_validated=false` and `causal_CADA_effect_established=false`. Completed assignments, recorded deadlines, and tracked events are not valid measures of academic quality.

New projects use `cada_science_boundary_required=true`. Previous projects only migrate after an explicit decision: to preserve their records and not infer that a historical note is technical proof.

<a id="protocolo-comparativo-preparado-sem-execução-empírica"></a>
## Comparative protocol: prepared, without empirical execution

The `benchmarks/cada_comparison_protocol_v1.json` file is the prospective protocol for future comparison **with C.A.D.A. / without C.A.D.A.**, with application to administrative research episodes that are comparable.

The two conditions must maintain **the same research object, corpus, search protocol, criteria, critical human decisions and standards of scientific validation**. The intervention that varies is the management and monitoring layer; the absence of C.A.D.A. does not mean dispensing with scientific records and controls.

The protocol contains:- unit: documented administrative demand with person responsible, action and verifiable evidence of conclusion;
- design: paired episodes and prospective allocation, according to feasibility; do not attribute causality to differences in convenience;
- administrative results: time until first action, time until discharge, documented rework and percentage of deadlines not met;
- scientific safety outcomes: adherence to the protocol, claims support errors and screening quality assessed **independently**;
- risks: selection of easier tasks, difference in tools, learning, contamination of conditions, additional work and loss of records;
- analysis: define estimates, exclusion criteria, treatment of missing data, uncertainty intervals and divergences before the first episode.

The protocol **does not contain observations or estimates**. No experiments were performed and no efficiency or scientific quality advantages were demonstrated. Result fields were deliberately left null/empty. Do not insert fictitious numbers to complete the matrix.

<a id="limitações"></a>
## Limitations

Automated testing only checks records, structural consistency and inadmissibility of certain shortcuts; it does not authenticate human beings, does not directly observe the execution of external tasks and does not measure managerial benefit.

The C.A.D.A. It can, as a hypothesis to be tested, reduce delay, rework and information asymmetry. Efficacy depends on empirical research with its own evidence and adequate comparative design. Until then, describe it as a **proposed function** or **operational capacity of the software**, never as a proven effect.

Management reports can be shared separately from scientific results to prevent administrative indicators from becoming undue proof of academic rigor. No tabs, identifier families or paid integrations were created.
