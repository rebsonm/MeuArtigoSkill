## Features and source material

- Seven researcher-controlled scientific gates remain separate from software tasks; [L] consulted primary-source evidence, [I] interpretations and [P] proposals are distinguished.
- Project initialization, twenty CSV tables, continuity/protocol files, resumption safeguards, traceable DEC/TRACE and source-claim boundaries.
- Read-only previews across eight methodological routes, conservative next-step guidance, permission-aware Google Drive workflow and hash-based readback.
- **649 cards:** 475 bibliographic prospecting records [P] and 174 methodological editorial cards [I]/[P]; no automatic promotion to scientific evidence.
- **51 academic PDFs** remain under their authors' and publishers' individual CC BY conditions and are credited in [PDF-ATTRIBUTIONS.md](../knowledge/methodology/PDF-ATTRIBUTIONS.md). They do not inherit the software's Apache-2.0 license.

## Real-host functional report (2026-11-22)

The maintainer reported PASS for fictional project creation with eight metadata fields and twenty CSV tables; two methodology cards with honest [L]/[I]/[P] labels; 28 file hashes matching on write/readback plus the receipt; and preservation of all seven PENDING gates, zero approved scientific decisions and zero research publication.

**Optional XLSX failed** because the tested host lacked a dependency; the canonical CSV files were preserved. Earlier byte-exact T02 installation checks failed for two host-presentation files (151/153); dedicated installed-retrieval T32 and installed-resume T34 were NOT_RUN. Neither a GitHub release nor this report overrides those limitations.
