# Meu Artigo experimental beta: information for researchers

Meu Artigo is being tested as a **Skill for scientific research support**. You can explore its guidance and workflow without claiming that it has been independently validated or that it guarantees a publishable article.

## Available download

The **confirmed published experimental installer** is [v0.9.0-beta.6](https://github.com/rebsonm/MeuArtigoSkill/releases/tag/v0.9.0-beta.6), with the [prepared ZIP](https://github.com/rebsonm/MeuArtigoSkill/releases/download/v0.9.0-beta.6/MeuArtigoSkill-v0.9.0-beta.6.zip) and SHA-256 attachment. Unmerged PR changes remain development candidates, not part of the published package.

For installation, always choose the prepared ZIP on the [Releases page](https://github.com/rebsonm/MeuArtigoSkill/releases), not **Code → Download ZIP**. Each installed Skill remains on its installed version until you update it.

[Read the step-by-step installation guide](INSTALLATION.md).

## What can researchers try?

- Describe a research problem and compare suitable methodological approaches.
- Plan searches and review actual records from sources you are allowed to access.
- Keep scientific decisions, supporting evidence, tasks and deadlines organized.
- Close the conversation and later continue an existing article from its saved project.
- See what the assistant can verify, what needs your decision and what is currently blocked.

You can do this in your preferred conversational language. A research method or journal style should not be imposed merely because it is convenient for the software.

## Services, access and privacy

Google Drive is the preferred canonical storage only after its permissions and writable access are actually verified. A local alternative requires your explicit agreement. Paid databases, full-text content, cloud synchronization and publication services are not bundled with the Skill.

Avoid sharing access credentials, confidential manuscripts, protected participant details or third-party PDFs unless their use is authorized.

## What has not been demonstrated?

Engineering tests are available in the source repository, but they are not a substitute for independent scientific evaluation or broad real-user testing. The quality of a particular article, the semantic correctness of its claims, and compatibility with every assistant or external service still require real checks.
