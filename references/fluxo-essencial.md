# Essential research flow — start without overwhelming the user

**Minimum input:** an authentic research problem or idea. Do not require
the researcher to know databases, statistics, review methods, Boolean
searches, spreadsheets or target journals at the outset.

## Observable sequence

1. **Secure storage before research.** Verify actual Google Drive
   read/write access. If unavailable after the connection attempt,
   require the user's explicit affirmative permission for
   `WORK_FALLBACK`, recording who authorized it and when. Do not
   conduct substantive research during blocked storage onboarding.
2. **Preserve the question.** Keep the original text and offer a
   provisional question, contribution and limitation.
3. **Choose an appropriate design.** Distinguish literature review,
   theoretical article, qualitative, quantitative, mixed-method
   and design science studies. Do not generate an impersonated
   researcher answer. Use `UNDECIDED` while the scientific choice
   is unresolved. See [method routes](../docs/ROTAS-METODOLOGICAS.md).
4. **Plan only what the design requires.** Bibliographic reviews
   may need reproducible searching, actual retrieval, eligibility,
   critical appraisal and synthesis. Empirical designs need
   actual data/material acquisition, analysis procedures and
   design-specific quality criteria. Mixed methods need real
   QUAN–QUAL integration, and design science separates artefact
   construction from evaluation.
5. **Act only when capability and authorization permit.** Record
   executed, pending, blocked and unverified actions separately.
   Never invent database access, observations or empirical results.
6. **Show the researcher only what matters:** the next consequential
   human decision, the next feasible action and a salient risk
   or missing piece of evidence. Keep fuller records available.
7. **Preserve continuity.** Update `CONTINUIDADE.md` and the
   canonical evidence/decision state in the authorized workspace.
8. **Before submitting:** review sources and inferences, actual
   journal rules, privacy, copyright, AI-use disclosure and the
   exact files. Claim submission only after external confirmation.

The C.A.D.A. matrix, source ledger and provenance logs remain
canonical, but they are **not an initial researcher questionnaire**.
Do not add sheets/ID families or interrupt the user with purely
operational questions.

## Language at first contact and throughout the session

All these **source instructions are English**, but the assistant's
questions and responses follow the researcher's current substantive
direct-message language. A Portuguese-speaking researcher can
use the Skill in Portuguese immediately without selecting a locale.
The manuscript language is a separate scientific/editorial choice.
Consult [language policy](language-policy.md).

## Example of the minimal interaction pattern

“I can now carry out **X**. Before proceeding, your scientific
decision about **Y** is required because **Z** is an important
limitation.”

This is an interaction example — never fill in a scientific decision
on behalf of a real researcher. Read [beginner support](beginner-mode.md)
only when it is actually needed. Translate this example naturally into
the user's language when speaking to them.
