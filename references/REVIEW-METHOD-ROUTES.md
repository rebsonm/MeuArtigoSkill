# Research review-method decision guide — Snyder, Marzi, SALSA, Salvador and PRISMA

**Status:** an original, provisional method-choice guide. The investigator selects the study design, records DEC/TRACE decisions and verifies the actual evidence. No checklist or software automatically approves a scientific protocol or gate.

| Research problem and intended contribution | Candidate approach | Decisions to preserve | Do not claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Estimate or compare outcomes across a bounded evidence base | Systematic review, with or without meta-analysis | Eligibility, reproducible searches, synthesis decisions, bias appraisal, suitable PRISMA reporting | That a short or selective search is exhaustive |
| Map evidence types, terminology or research gaps | Scoping review | Mapping question, source selection, data charting, suitable PRISMA-ScR reporting | That descriptive mapping proves intervention effectiveness |
| Understand the evolution of a heterogeneous field | Semisystematic review (Snyder) | Transparent perimeter, justified corpus sampling, temporal or thematic interpretation, limitations | That using a PRISMA-looking diagram makes the approach systematic |
| Reframe established theory across academic traditions | Integrative review (Snyder; Elsbach and van Knippenberg) | Theoretical boundaries, contradictions, comparison and a genuinely new perspective | That descriptive article summaries constitute theoretical synthesis |
| Map knowledge structures and systematically synthesize selected texts | B-SLR (Marzi et al.) | Ten methodological steps, checkpoints, bibliometric parameters, reading, interpretation and theory-building | That visual clusters alone establish a theory |
| Compare families of review approaches | SALSA taxonomy (Grant and Booth) | Search, Appraisal, Synthesis and Analysis for each design | That SALSA is an external research-quality certificate |
| Investigate literature through critical source reading | Bibliographic research (Salvador, historical reference) | Research boundaries, critical reading, source criticism and integrative argument | Direct consultation of Salvador's book, which was not located in this source review |
| Develop theory without a newly collected empirical dataset | Conceptual article (Jaakkola) | Theory synthesis, adaptation, typology or model, with novel implications | That all narrative or conceptual texts are rigorous theory contributions |
| Describe the conduct of a database search transparently | PRISMA-S as a reporting companion | Exact database platform, search string, date, filters, exported records and decision trail | That a reporting extension substitutes for a full methodological design |

Select the route by the research question, inferential purpose and available evidence, not by tools such as Bibliometrix, VOSviewer, Rayyan or ChatGPT. Computational tools are optional operational supports, not epistemological commitments.
