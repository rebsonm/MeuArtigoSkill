# Meu Artigo — Privacy policy

Publisher: Rebson de Morais Mendes. Applies to the skills-only Meu Artigo plugin.

## Data processing
The publisher does not operate a server that collects or stores researchers’ project data through this plugin. The plugin provides packaged instructions and support files. Prompts, uploaded documents, generated content and tool activity may be processed by the host platform according to its own terms, privacy policy and user settings.

## Connected services and storage
If the researcher authorizes external tools such as Google Drive, bibliographic databases or GitHub, those services may process or store information under their own policies. Research files may be saved in the researcher's authorized workspace. Requests to public repositories or literature providers may disclose technical request information to those providers. Do not include confidential or personal information in unnecessary external queries.

## Control and retention
The publisher has no separate plugin-operated database of researchers’ project information to retain or delete. Manage host conversations, connected services and stored research files using the relevant provider controls. Information voluntarily posted in public GitHub issues is visible to others and is governed by GitHub's terms and policies. Do not post sensitive research materials there.
