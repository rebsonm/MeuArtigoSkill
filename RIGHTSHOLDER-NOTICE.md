**Meu Artigo is an independently maintained, non-commercial research-support
project.** Its purpose is to help researchers learn about methods, discover
legitimate academic sources, and document their own scientific decisions. The
project does not claim endorsement by the authors, journals, or publishers
whose works are cited or linked.

## Notice to authors and other rights holders

If you are an author, publisher, editor, or other rights holder and believe
that a work or its presentation here should be corrected or removed,
**please email [rebsonm@gmail.com](mailto:rebsonm@gmail.com)**.

Please identify the work (title and/or DOI), the relevant GitHub file or
release link, your concern, and whether you request corrected attribution,
a change of presentation, withdrawal of a downloadable copy, or removal.
A brief statement of your connection to the work is useful; do not post
confidential identification records or private correspondence in public
issues. Good-faith requests will be reviewed promptly, and material will
be corrected, restricted, or taken down where warranted. The maintainer
may request clarification or evidence of rights where necessary.
