# Methodological routes

Meu Artigo does **not** prescribe one universal method. The researcher's problem, inferential goal and available legitimate evidence determine a **proposed** route; a real researcher confirms or revises it.

| Route | Typical purpose | Essential caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Integrative review | Critically combine bounded literature | Do not claim exhaustive coverage |
| Systematic review | Answer a protocol-defined evidence question | PRISMA vocabulary does not itself make a review systematic |
| Problematizing review | Challenge assumptions in prior arguments | Do not force exhaustive screening without justification |
| Conceptual/theoretical | Develop a framework, mechanism or proposition | Differentiate original contribution from nearby literature |
| Qualitative | Understand meanings, processes or context | Use tradition-appropriate reflexivity and quality criteria |
| Quantitative | Describe, associate, predict or causally estimate | Causal claims require defensible identification assumptions |
| Mixed methods | Integrate substantial qualitative and quantitative strands | Parallel strands alone are not integration |
| Design science | Build and assess an artefact | Construction is not evidence of effectiveness |

The initial choice can remain **`UNDECIDED`** while rationale and alternatives are explored. The assistant must not fabricate a researcher decision simply to advance.

For suitable studies, the **research onion** is a coherence check across epistemic assumptions, theory-development logic, choice of methods, strategy, time horizon and procedures. It is not a deterministic quiz that imposes a philosophical paradigm.

## Examples of method-sensitive decisions

**Qualitative:** describe the actual case/phenomenon, sampling or documentary selection, interpretive tradition, analysis and reflexive position. No universal requirement for Kappa.

**Quantitative:** distinguish descriptive, associative, predictive and causal claims; specify units, data, measurements and analyses actually performed.

**Mixed methods:** state why integration is necessary, where it happens and how discordant findings are interpreted. Do not call an unexecuted integration plan a result.

**Design science:** separate requirements, artefact construction, evaluation design, observed evaluation and limits.

**Reviews:** match search, eligibility, screening, appraisal and synthesis to the selected review family.
