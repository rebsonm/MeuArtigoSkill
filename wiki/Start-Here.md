# Start here

You can use Meu Artigo whether your research question is already defined or still an idea. The Skill should suggest possible ways forward without choosing your method for you.

## Five simple steps

1. **Install the published Skill** if your assistant supports Skills. See [[Installation]].
2. **Describe your research problem** in your own language.
3. **Compare methodological options.** Ask why each is suitable, what evidence it needs and what its limitations are.
4. **Confirm storage and actual sources.** The assistant should only claim a saved file, database search or imported article when it can verify that action.
5. **Decide and proceed.** Your research choices matter. The assistant can organize work and explain the next step, but not make those decisions in your place.

## A first message you can copy

> I want to write an article about [my topic]. Help me identify a researchable question and compare suitable methods. First check what tools and storage are actually available, and tell me what I will need to decide myself.

If you are returning to an article, say which one. Meu Artigo should consult accessible project records instead of rebuilding previous decisions from guesswork.

The service may answer in Portuguese or another language you prefer. Journal and manuscript requirements are separate from the conversation language.
