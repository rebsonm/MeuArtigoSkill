# Seven scientific gates

**A gate requires a real, accountable researcher's substantive decision.** The Skill can prepare materials, check recorded dependencies and propose options, but it cannot manufacture approval or impersonate a second independent reviewer.

| Gate | Consequential decision | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GATE-0001` | Question and intended contribution | Original formulation, scope, feasibility |
| `GATE-0002` | Research design and protocol | Appropriate route, researcher's rationale, trade-offs |
| `GATE-0003` | Literature/search or theoretical positioning | Actual strategy and provenance relevant to the route |
| `GATE-0004` | Eligible evidence or real materials | Bibliographic corpus **or** empirical materials/data/artefact, as applicable |
| `GATE-0005` | Synthesis, analysis or evaluation | Performed procedures, output, discrepancies, limitations |
| `GATE-0006` | Integrity of claims and interpretation | Claim provenance, contrary evidence, bounded novelty |
| `GATE-0007` | Journal/submission readiness | Official rules, anonymization, disclosures, verifiable submission actions |

## How an approval should work

1. Present the specific question and the **actual evidence** that supports the decision.
2. Identify unresolved risks, inconsistencies, exclusions or limitations.
3. Obtain the **researcher's substantive response** with attributable rationale.
4. Record the decision using existing `GATE_ID`, `DEC_ID` and trace references where applicable.
5. Preserve a real project snapshot when the workflow requires freezing state.

A user saying "next" or "proceed" is not automatically an informed scientific gate approval. An AI proposal, a spreadsheet status change, a plausible generated audit log or a C.A.D.A. `DONE` value is not independent evidence of approval.

**Special cases:** Gate 4 and Gate 5 mean different things for a literature review, interviews, quantitative analyses, mixed methods and design science. A journal-free project can leave Gate 7 pending; it should not be artificially marked complete.
