# Status and limitations

## What is available

- A **published experimental pre-release** [v0.9.0-beta.4](https://github.com/rebsonm/MeuArtigoSkill/releases/tag/v0.9.0-beta.4), including its prepared Skill installer and checksum.
- A versioned scientific contract, route-specific references, C.A.D.A. workflow, continuity and provenance controls.
- Deterministic software tests and developer-facing audits in the source repository.
- Documentation for supported/possible platforms, manual source workflows, lawful rights handling and user feedback.

## What is **not** established by the release

- Independent scientific validation of the Skill or a complete research methodology.
- Demonstrated causal improvements in productivity, article quality or administrative efficiency from C.A.D.A.
- Actual external user studies, a verified complete article evaluation or universal journal compliance.
- Verified end-to-end execution on every assistant or live multilingual compatibility.
- Access to paid databases, private full text, Google Drive authorization or submission on behalf of an individual user.
- Proof of source quality, human identity or scholarly originality merely from passing software tests.

**Engineering regression tests are not equivalent to a field study.** A local action receipt is not independent evidence of an external provider's action. The project must continue marking pending evaluations as **PENDING** until actually completed and documented.

## Contributions and feedback

Voluntary beta feedback should report reproducible behavior and avoid personal/confidential materials. Claims made for a specific installed account, tool connection or completed scientific gate must be supported by the actual session's observed results.
