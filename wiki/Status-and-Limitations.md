# What Meu Artigo can and cannot do

**Meu Artigo is experimental research-support software, not an independently validated scientific method.**

## What it is designed to help with

- Turning an initial problem into a more focused research question.
- Understanding and comparing methodological alternatives.
- Organizing actual sources, evidence and analysis.
- Keeping an article's decisions, tasks and pending steps accessible.
- Preparing writing for review against genuine journal requirements.

## What has not been established

- That it produces scientifically correct or original articles without expert review.
- That it saves time, improves article quality or increases publication acceptance.
- That every suggested claim accurately reflects a source without checking it.
- That every platform, account, database or connected storage service works equally.
- That a successful technical test proves independent scientific validity.

An assistant can say that a step is planned, completed or unverified only when the available evidence supports that status. You remain responsible for research ethics, source rights and scientific decisions.
