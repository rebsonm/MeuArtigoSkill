## Licensing and public installation constraints

- The 36 currently indexed public original PDFs are listed separately with source URL, bibliographic reference, license evidence, file size and SHA-256 in `knowledge/methodology/pdf_catalog.json`. Their published source bits stay outside the installed Skill ZIP.
- A source under CC BY-NC or CC BY-NC-SA may permit some noncommercial sharing, but this **public multi-user integration deliberately does not treat them as unrestricted CC BY 4.0 originals**. The exact license and its restrictions must follow any future authorized distribution.
- For protected or uncertain works, the available path is **citation metadata, lawful original link, and genuinely independent short editorial cards**. Do not substitute serial summaries, PDF-to-MD conversions, or copied figures for a copyrighted work.
- An indexed record, an original card or a SHA-256 verification is not an approval of the scientific method, evidence grade, researcher gate, or research outcome.
- The 475-item register records **source coverage**, not 475 fully read sources. Only source-specific completed rights checks are counted as publisher-verified.
