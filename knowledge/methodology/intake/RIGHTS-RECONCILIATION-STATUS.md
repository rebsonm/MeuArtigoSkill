## What cannot be claimed

- That all copyright-free/open licensed articles among the 475 have been
  identified or uploaded.
- That the 12 public PDFs represent 12 of the 475 archive works; six may be
  external additions, and source names are not identity proofs.
- That the remaining archive is closed access, restricted, private rights, or
  unusable: the vast majority are **not individually reviewed yet**.
- That 23 cards cover all methodological topics or that all individual books
  and articles have been independently read. Card drafting is not card
  scientific acceptance.
- That a PDF downloadable without a password is automatically permitted for
  public GitHub redistribution.

## Required follow-up, document by document

Build a source-level rights ledger with: exact source filename, work identity
(title, authors, DOI and version), accessible original URL, public full-text
license or absent license, allowed redistribution determination, evidence URL,
reviewer/date, upload outcome and PDF SHA256 when approved. Only upload a PDF
where the original **exact file** may be redistributed. For protected works,
publish only genuinely original editorial cards after independent rights and
scientific fidelity review, avoiding serial substitutes for the source.
Track unmatched documents explicitly as UNKNOWN, not as denied or completed.

Status remains **INCOMPLETE**. No merge, release, full-text bulk ingestion,
unreviewed redistribution, scientific gate approval or private Drive copying
is authorized by this ledger.
