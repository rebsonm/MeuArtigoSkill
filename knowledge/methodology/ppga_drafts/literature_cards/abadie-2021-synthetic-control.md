## Method-selection question [P]

Can a weighted donor pool reconstruct credible untreated pre-intervention outcomes?

## Proposed decision process [P]

Predefine intervention, donor eligibility, adequate pre-period, fit assessment and placebo sensitivity.

## Counterexample or misuse boundary [P]

A visually good post-treatment divergence without acceptable pre-treatment support is not convincing causal evidence.
