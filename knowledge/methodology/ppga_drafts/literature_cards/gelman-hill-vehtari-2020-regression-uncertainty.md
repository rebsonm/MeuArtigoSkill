## Method-selection question [P]

Does the fitted statistical model describe the observed data adequately for the intended decision?

## Proposed decision process [P]

Inspect residual patterns, predictive calibration, variation across groups and interpretable effect magnitudes before concluding.

## Counterexample or misuse boundary [P]

Good in-sample fit can conceal model misspecification, extrapolation and unstable subgroup claims.
