## Methodological question [P]

Which project's operational readiness conditions are necessary for AI to create usable value rather than merely be adopted?

## Original study-design checkpoint [P]

Distinguish AI availability, process readiness, staff capability, governance capacity and value realisation. Define a counterfactual or clear baseline for any claimed benefit.

## Falsification / inference boundary [P]

Tool deployment, AI use frequency or perceived efficiency are not sufficient evidence that a project produced net value.
