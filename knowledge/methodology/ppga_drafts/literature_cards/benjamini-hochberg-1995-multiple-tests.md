## Method-selection question [P]

## Proposed decision process [P]

Declare the family of tests and select a justified false-discovery-rate or familywise-error procedure with dependence assumptions.

## Counterexample or misuse boundary [P]

Testing many outcomes and reporting only those with unadjusted p below 0.05 inflates misleading positives.
