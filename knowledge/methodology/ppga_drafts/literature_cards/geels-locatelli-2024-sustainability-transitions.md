## Method-selection question [P]

What project type and system level best describe the studied transition?

## Proposed decision process [P]

Differentiate exploratory, improvement, deployment, reorientation and decommissioning projects and define the system boundary.

## Counterexample or misuse boundary [P]

One successful project does not by itself demonstrate a sociotechnical system transition.
