## Method-selection question [P]

Does the interpretation rely on a p-value cutoff instead of magnitude, design and uncertainty?

## Proposed decision process [P]

Report effect estimates, interval uncertainty, assumptions, design limitations and practical importance alongside p-values.

## Counterexample or misuse boundary [P]

p below 0.05 does not imply a hypothesis is true or an effect is important.
