## Research question / strategy choice [P]

When do temporal narratives, stage comparisons, process maps or quantification justify a proposed explanation?

## Proposed procedure [P]

Map events, actors, sequence, change mechanisms and alternative causal accounts; compare at least two analytic approaches.

## Failure mode or counterexample [P]

Treating successive events as proof of a process mechanism without alternative tests is inadequate.
