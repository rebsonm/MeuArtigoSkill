## Method-selection question [P]

Should the paper extend a gap or challenge a hidden assumption in an established literature?

## Proposed decision process [P]

List the literature's baseline assumptions, articulate the problem with one assumption and formulate a question that changes possible explanation rather than merely changing population.

## Counterexample or misuse boundary [P]

A rhetorical challenge without an alternative empirical or conceptual route does not establish theoretical contribution.
