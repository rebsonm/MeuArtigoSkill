## Method-selection question [P]

Which plan-driven and agile elements fit project uncertainty and control needs?

## Proposed decision process [P]

Choose practices by project phase and decision conditions; record coordination trade-offs and define outcomes for evaluating a hybrid arrangement.

## Counterexample or misuse boundary [P]

Mixing labels such as agile and waterfall without a project-specific configuration does not create a defensible hybrid method.
