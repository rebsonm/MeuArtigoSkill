## Method selection question

Can online group interaction illuminate a social construction process?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

Plan platform, facilitation, group size, discussion dynamics, anonymity boundaries and data capture.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Review moderator influence, unequal participation, consent, group confidentiality and technology exclusion.

## Falsification / boundary example

A chat survey answered individually is not a focus group.
