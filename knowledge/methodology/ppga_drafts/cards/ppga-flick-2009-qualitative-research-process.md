## Methodological decision question

How can a qualitative research question govern choices from epistemology through observation/interview, text production, analysis and reporting?

## Independent decision guidance

Design a chain connecting research question, participant or site selection, data generation, transformations into analyzable material, interpretation and the intended kind of explanation. A qualitative design is not defined solely by having interview transcripts.

## Researcher checks and falsification opportunities

Identify the knowledge claim; justify the chosen qualitative tradition and data form; document transformations, analytic decisions, ethical safeguards and rival explanations.

## Competing design / counterexample

A one-off descriptive survey with free-text responses does not automatically become a grounded, interpretive qualitative study.
