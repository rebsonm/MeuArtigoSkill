## Methodological decision question

How should organizational research connect philosophical assumptions, evidence and managerial relevance?

## Independent decision guidance

Before selecting interviews or observations, state the interpretive, critical or other knowledge position and assess whether the phenomenon is best understood through situated meaning, organizational processes or more formal comparisons.

## Researcher checks and falsification opportunities

State how access, field relationships, evidence selection, interpretation and ethical disclosure may shape the finding; distinguish practical relevance from methodological evidence.

## Competing design / counterexample

A qualitative organizational case is not a convenient substitute for an unfeasible causal impact estimate.
