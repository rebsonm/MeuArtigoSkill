## Methodological decision question

Which specific evidence could contradict or refine the claim the question invites?

## Independent decision guidance

Trace the question to the target inference, construct definition, sampling/data structure, measurement, analysis, rival explanations and the maximum defensible contribution. Cross-sectional observations do not automatically identify changes over time or causal effects.

## Researcher checks and falsification opportunities

Check question–design alignment, construct validity, model specification, common-method issues, selection mechanisms and unrealistically strong claims.

## Competing design / counterexample

Do not propose a causal effect of a program if there is no plausible comparison, timing or identification strategy.
