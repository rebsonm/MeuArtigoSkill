## Method selection question

Does variation within units over time address the proposed association or causal question?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

Specify unit and time identifiers, treatment variation, unit and period effects and the source of identifying variation.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Review clustered errors, temporal confounding, staggered timing, missing panels, serial dependence and selection.

## Falsification / boundary example

Adding unit fixed effects does not correct every time-varying omitted variable.
