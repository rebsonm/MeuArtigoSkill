## Method selection question

What changes in qualitative interview evidence when interaction is remote?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

Choose synchronous/asynchronous mode and record how platform, access and rapport influence participant accounts.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Address privacy, secure recording, consent, accessibility, interruptions, recruitment and nonverbal cues.

## Falsification / boundary example

Remote convenience does not imply equivalence to face-to-face evidence.
