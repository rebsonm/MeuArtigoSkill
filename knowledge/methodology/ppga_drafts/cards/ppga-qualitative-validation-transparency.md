## Method selection question

How should the quality standard follow the study's epistemology and method?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

Plan an auditable record of sampling, field relationships, interpretation, contradictions and reporting choices.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Distinguish reflexive quality, coding reproducibility, transferability and replicability instead of forcing one universal metric.

## Falsification / boundary example

High inter-coder agreement is not always the appropriate validity criterion for reflexive thematic analysis.
