## Method selection question

Which regression assumptions and estimation targets make the coefficient interpretable?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

State model purpose, measurement levels, error dependence, functional form and covariate selection rationale.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Inspect residuals, heteroskedasticity, influence, missingness, multicollinearity and predictive versus causal uses.

## Falsification / boundary example

A high R-squared does not validate model assumptions or causal estimates.
