## Method selection question

Do observed items support a plausible latent measurement structure?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

Separate exploratory structure discovery from confirmatory testing, and define the construct before extracting components.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Assess sampling adequacy, extraction/rotation, cross-loadings, interpretability, reliability and replication sample.

## Falsification / boundary example

A software-default number of factors does not demonstrate construct validity.
