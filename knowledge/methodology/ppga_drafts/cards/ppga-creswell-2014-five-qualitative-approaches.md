## Methodological decision question

Which research tradition fits the phenomenon and intended contribution?

## Independent decision guidance

Compare narrative inquiry (experience as story), phenomenology (the meaning of lived experience), grounded theory (developing categories and explanation), ethnography (shared cultural practices) and case study (a bounded case). Choose a tradition by the research question and what counts as adequate evidence, not by fashion.

## Researcher checks and falsification opportunities

Define unit of analysis, researcher's role, sampling logic, data form, analytic moves and quality criteria appropriate to the selected approach.

## Competing design / counterexample

Calling five interviews a case study without a bounded case and multiple relevant evidential perspectives is a category mistake.
