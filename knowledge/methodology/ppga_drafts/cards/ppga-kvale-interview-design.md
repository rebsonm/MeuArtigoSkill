## Methodological decision question

What interview evidence is suited to a claim about participants' accounts and meaning?

## Independent decision guidance

Prepare a pilotable interview rationale: sampling, interaction setting, semi-structured themes, consent, recording, transcription fidelity, reflexive memos and analysis.

## Researcher checks and falsification opportunities

Assess social desirability, interviewer influence, translation, privacy and discrepancy between reported action and observed practice.

## Competing design / counterexample

An interviewer's leading question cannot independently establish the participant's unprompted position.
