## Methodological decision question

Can the proposed article's question, theory, method and contribution fit on one falsifiable logic map?

## Independent decision guidance

Use a canvas as a diagnostic conversation: problem, assumption, claim, evidence, research strategy, sample, analysis, plausible rival explanation, limits and intended contribution. Treat disagreements as open design issues.

## Researcher checks and falsification opportunities

Require each box to connect to its neighbors; keep unresolved assumptions explicit; avoid formatting quality being mistaken for scientific validity.

## Competing design / counterexample

A well-filled research canvas is not an approved research protocol or evidence that a study is feasible.
