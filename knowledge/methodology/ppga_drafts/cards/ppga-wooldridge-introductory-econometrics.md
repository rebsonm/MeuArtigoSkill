## Methodological decision question

Does the model identify the desired parameter under plausible assumptions?

## Independent decision guidance

Predefine unit, outcome, predictor/treatment, time dimension, confounders, missingness, selection and the relationship between statistical association and causal identification.

## Researcher checks and falsification opportunities

Evaluate functional form, omitted-variable risk, standard-error structure, clustering, sensitivity and credible counterfactuals.

## Competing design / counterexample

A significant coefficient in a cross-sectional observational regression does not automatically establish causality.
