## Methodological decision question

What would make grounded theory appropriate to a managerial process explanation?

## Independent decision guidance

Define an emergent conceptual question, collect rich organizational evidence, compare incidents, refine categories and keep memos on how explanations change.

## Competing design / counterexample

Labeling ordinary open coding as grounded theory without category development is methodologically weak.
