## Methodological decision question

What operational plan makes a qualitative study both flexible and auditable?

## Independent decision guidance

Differentiate a flexible design from an unspecified design. Establish a problem statement, participant/site rationale, data collection practices, analytic iteration and explicit quality/evidence boundaries while allowing justified amendments.

## Researcher checks and falsification opportunities

Record changes to the sampling plan, interviewer/observer role, interview or observation records, coding memos, case boundaries and confidentiality.

## Competing design / counterexample

An immutable pre-registered interview count may not fit a design whose conceptual sampling develops iteratively; flexibility does not excuse undocumented improvisation.
