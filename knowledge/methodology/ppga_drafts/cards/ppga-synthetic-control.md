## Method selection question

Can a weighted donor comparison approximate the treated unit absent intervention?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

State the treated unit and intervention timing, eligible donors, pretreatment fit, contextual shocks and the target effect.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Assess donor contamination, pre/post trajectories, placebo analyses, specification sensitivity and uncertainty limitations.

## Falsification / boundary example

A donor-weighted curve with weak pretreatment fit is not a reliable counterfactual.
