## Method selection question

Can a credible instrument isolate exogenous exposure variation?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

State relevance, exclusion restriction, independence and the population for which an instrumental estimate could apply.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Test first-stage strength and sensitivity; openly justify exclusion using institutional knowledge, not only statistical tests.

## Falsification / boundary example

Instrument strength alone cannot validate the exclusion restriction.
