## Method selection question

Can observed covariates support a more comparable observational contrast?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

Specify baseline covariates, common support and what matching is expected to improve before outcome inspection.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Report balance, overlap, unit loss, target population shift and unobserved-confounding sensitivity.

## Falsification / boundary example

Balanced observed covariates do not eliminate unobserved selection.
