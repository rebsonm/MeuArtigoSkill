## Method selection question

How does an effect or association vary by a moderator?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

Write the estimand as a conditional effect, state the scale on which interaction is meant and assess whether the moderator is pretreatment.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Present marginal effects with uncertainty and observed support; review main-term interpretation, collinearity and extrapolation.

## Falsification / boundary example

A significant product term does not imply the same effect modification on every outcome scale.
