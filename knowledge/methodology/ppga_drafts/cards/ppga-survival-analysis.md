## Method selection question

Is the response a duration to a defined event with incomplete observation?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

Specify origin time, event, risk set, time-varying covariates and censoring assumptions.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Review competing events, proportional-hazards diagnostics where applicable, left truncation and informative censoring.

## Falsification / boundary example

Treating all non-events as zero-duration failures biases time-to-event interpretation.
