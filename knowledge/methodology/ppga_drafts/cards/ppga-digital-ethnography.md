## Method selection question

What online interactions constitute the field and its culture?

## Proposed methodological decision logic [P]

Treat platforms, participation, visibility rules, digital traces and ethical expectations as parts of fieldwork.

## Checks before committing to this strategy

Document observation period, situated context, public/private expectations, consent and researcher positionality.

## Falsification / boundary example

Scraping public posts alone does not constitute immersive ethnography.
