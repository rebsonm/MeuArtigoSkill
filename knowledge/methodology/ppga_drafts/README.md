# PPGA methodology draft intake

This directory contains **50 staged methodology card candidates** (21 source/author-oriented + 29 strategy/topic-oriented) prepared from a PPGA course-library inventory. It is intentionally separate from `knowledge/methodology/cards/` and `catalog.json`: these are **not** part of the 174 active editorial cards or 475 existing bibliographic prospecting cards.

Eight cards report PARTIAL_DIRECT_READING of named source portions or course slides. The other 42 cards are metadata-only [P] original decision prompts. No source PDF, direct quotation, private Drive identifier, paid chapter, classroom slide or publisher figure is republished. Each candidate requires edition-level source verification and two distinct reviews (rights/originality and scientific/editorial fidelity) before any promotion.

See [intake and coverage ledger](./PPGA-METHODOLOGY-INVENTORY-2026-11-22.md). Researcher-controlled scientific gates and decisions remain unchanged.
