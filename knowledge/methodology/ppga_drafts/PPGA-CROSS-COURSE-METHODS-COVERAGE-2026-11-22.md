# PPGA methodology content from other academic courses (22 November 2026)

**Primary-research-only citation policy:** Course slides or instructor presentations are inventory *locators*, **not research references**. All new methodological cards cite original scholarly publications or books. No teaching file or privately held textbook full text is placed in this source audit.
