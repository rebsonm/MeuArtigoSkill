## Intended methodological question

What makes an LLM-generated discussion of documents different from a defensible methodological analysis?

## Original editorial guidance

Require analytic operations and inferential warrants independent of fluent generated prose. Specify the type of inference expected, expose relevant input boundaries and examine whether model output can be linked to actual passages and rival interpretations.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Record verifiable data-to-claim links, counterevidence, sensitivity to prompts, model limitations, omitted contexts and human checks on synthesized interpretations.

## Limitations and counterevidence

Conversational fluency, repeated output or apparent coherence are not substitutes for construct validity, theoretical interpretation or source examination.
