## Methodological decision

How can a reference list avoid claims unsupported by the cited documents?

## Independent editorial guidance

For each consequential claim, verify author, version, source passage, study design and citation role. Preserve contradictory evidence and corrections rather than treating citation counts as validity.

## What not to infer

A correct DOI alone is not source-content verification, and highly cited research can still be misrepresented.
