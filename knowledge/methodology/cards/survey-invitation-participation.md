## Methodological decision

Does increasing responses necessarily reduce bias?

## Independent editorial guidance

Define the response denominator and contact protocol before data collection. Distinguish accessible respondents from the target population, track who cannot be reached, and examine whether mail versus web mode changes composition.

## What not to infer

A high overall response rate can coexist with systematic undercoverage of analytically important groups.
