## Methodological decision

When is referral sampling capable of supporting more than exploratory observations?

## Independent editorial guidance

Differentiate convenient snowball recruiting from respondent-driven sampling based on network assumptions. Record entry seeds, referral waves, eligibility, connectivity and the assumptions required for weighted population estimates.

## What not to infer

Device access, social networks and selective referral behavior can invalidate population-level claims.
