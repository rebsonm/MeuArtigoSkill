# Grounded theory: iterative category development, not generic coding

## Methodological question

When the purpose is to develop an explanatory conceptual account grounded in data rather than only classify responses.

## Original conceptual guidance

A defensible grounded-theory design connects empirical incidents, constant comparison, analytic memos, evolving categories and sampling decisions. Variants differ in their epistemic commitments; choose a defensible tradition and name its implications.

## Researcher decision checks

Document analytic memos, category development, how evidence challenged emerging explanations and how sampling evolved. Distinguish theoretical sampling from a fixed convenience sample.

## Limits and counterexamples

Generic thematic coding is not automatically grounded theory. Do not invoke theoretical saturation solely because a preset number of interviews was reached.
