# Interrupted time series: separate an interruption from a causal effect

Card ID: interrupted-time-series-inference
Publication state: DRAFT_NEEDS_INDEPENDENT_EDITORIAL_AND_RIGHTS_REVIEW
Research evidence grade: [I] ORIGINAL_EDITORIAL_SYNTHESIS_ONLY

## Methodological question

When a longitudinal outcome was repeatedly measured before and after an intervention.

## Original conceptual guidance

An interrupted time-series design compares observed trajectories with assumptions about the counterfactual trend. Intervention date, baseline trend, seasonality, autocorrelation, lag and concurrent shocks affect the strength of the inference.

## Researcher decision checks

Justify the time resolution, intervention timing, relevant pre/post observations, model specification, alternative explanations and diagnostics. Pre-specify what would refute the hypothesized effect.

## Limits and counterexamples

A visible level or slope change is not independently causal evidence. Avoid attributing unrelated policy events or secular trends to the intervention without design support.
