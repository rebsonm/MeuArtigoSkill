## Intended methodological question

How should empirical studies of saturation influence sample planning without imposing a numerical rule on every qualitative study?

## Original editorial guidance

Separate detecting new topical codes from developing conceptual depth and meaning. Context, homogeneity and information richness determine whether a stopping heuristic is transferable. Treat reported ranges as conditioned observations rather than prospective guarantees.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Name the saturation definition, topic complexity, planned analytic approach, evidence on heterogeneity and procedure for deciding when further data remain valuable.
