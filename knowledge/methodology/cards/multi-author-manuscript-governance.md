## Methodological decision

How can a multi-author paper preserve responsibility for every substantive claim?

## Independent editorial guidance

Define editorial ownership, author contributions, review cycles and conflict resolution. Preserve manuscript versions and document contested analytical decisions through final approval.

## What not to infer

Agreement among coauthors does not make a citation accurate or a scientific result independently valid.
