## What the source presents (requires source conference to quote)

Comparing the use of Claude Sonnet 3.5 and ChatGPT-4o in the interpretation of transcripts of workshops on future infrastructure, with and without additional context.

## Proposition for Skill use [I]

Skill can ask for the context of scenario development, list contradictions between interpretations and register human participation.

## Scientific decision issues [P]

- O contexto extra alterou a interpretação?
- O pesquisador inspecionou temas inesperados ou erros?
- Is there a risk of confusing prospective scenarios with reliable prediction?

## Contraponto e delimitações

The exploratory design and model versions prevent the conclusion that AI is superior in all qualitative analyses.
