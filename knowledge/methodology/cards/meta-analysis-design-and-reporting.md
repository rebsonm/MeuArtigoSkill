# Meta-analysis: an estimand and compatibility review precede statistical pooling

Card ID: meta-analysis-design-and-reporting
Publication state: DRAFT_NEEDS_INDEPENDENT_EDITORIAL_AND_RIGHTS_REVIEW
Research evidence grade: [I] ORIGINAL_EDITORIAL_SYNTHESIS_ONLY

## Methodological question

When considering whether a set of primary studies supports a quantitative pooled estimate.

## Original conceptual guidance

Specify the estimand, eligible study designs, outcome comparability and extraction rules before selecting a pooling model. A pooled number is meaningful only when the effect measures, clinical or substantive constructs, dependencies and biases are sufficiently understood. Report heterogeneity and alternative assumptions rather than treating statistical precision as substantive certainty.

## Researcher decision checks

Document protocol or justified amendments, search and eligibility decisions, study-level estimates, effect-size transformation, dependency handling, risk of bias, model choice, sensitivity analyses and confidence in the body of evidence.

## Limits and counterexamples

A meta-analysis is not appropriate merely because studies have numbers. The ten-rules article is a general introduction, not a replacement for field-specific reporting and synthesis methods or source verification.
