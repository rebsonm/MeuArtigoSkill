## Intended methodological question

When are organizational files, archived correspondence and historical records legitimate empirical documents rather than mere background references?

## Original editorial guidance

Define the documentary corpus as evidence produced in a social and institutional context. Distinguish original documentary investigation from a literature review, and examine provenance, audience, purpose, authorship and conditions of preservation before coding contents.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Identify document origin, period, inclusion criteria, authenticity, institutional incentives, missing records and how rival interpretations will be tested.

## Limitations and counterevidence

Official records may reflect the organization's interests and exclude informal practices. Frequency of a statement is not proof that a described action occurred.
