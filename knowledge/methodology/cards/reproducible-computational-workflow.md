# Computational reproducibility: preserve a checkable chain from inputs to outputs

Card ID: reproducible-computational-workflow
Publication state: DRAFT_NEEDS_INDEPENDENT_EDITORIAL_AND_RIGHTS_REVIEW
Research evidence grade: [I] ORIGINAL_EDITORIAL_SYNTHESIS_ONLY

## Methodological question

When a study uses scripts, notebooks or automated operations that must be traceable and rerunnable.

## Original conceptual guidance

Treat reproducibility as an evidence-preservation obligation across raw inputs, code, environment, parameter choices and outputs. Version the actual transformation steps, keep non-public data controlled and record checksums or immutable identifiers when appropriate. A successful rerun verifies a computational path, not the scientific validity of the underlying research claim.

## Researcher decision checks

List input provenance, transformations, versioned code, dependencies, random seeds where relevant, exceptions, disclosure restrictions and a verification procedure. Record failed attempts rather than quietly replacing them.

## Limits and counterexamples

Reproducibility is not equivalent to independent replication, accuracy, causal validity or permission to redistribute private data. Domain-specific evidence and human review remain necessary.
