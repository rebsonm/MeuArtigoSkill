## Independent editorial guidance

Distinguish sampling-frame coverage, propensity to respond and mode-related answer patterns. Preserve mode assignment, group composition and measurement comparability when interpreting pooled or weighted results.

## What not to infer

An observed difference between web and paper means is not necessarily a pure response-mode effect.
