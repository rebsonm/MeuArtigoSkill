# Qualitative design and quality: choose evidence for the question

## Methodological question

When deciding between interviews, observation, document analysis and other qualitative approaches.

## Original conceptual guidance

A qualitative design should address the nature, context and interpretation of a phenomenon. Choose data collection and analytic strategies that can support the intended conclusion, considering reflexivity, purposive sampling, quality controls and the ethical context.

## Researcher decision checks

Document why this form of data is necessary, who or what is sampled, how interpretation is constrained by evidence and what counterevidence is examined. Use quality practices compatible with the chosen research tradition.

## Limits and counterexamples

Do not substitute a generic checklist for epistemic alignment, or assume any single technique such as member checking is mandatory or universally sufficient.
