# Writing a literature review without mistaking narrative for systematic evidence

Card ID: literature-review-writing-practice
Publication state: DRAFT_NEEDS_INDEPENDENT_EDITORIAL_AND_RIGHTS_REVIEW
Research evidence grade: [I] ORIGINAL_EDITORIAL_SYNTHESIS_ONLY

## Methodological question

When a researcher must craft a scholarly literature review that supports a clearly bounded question rather than merely listing papers.

## Original conceptual guidance

Define what the review must contribute before selecting or narrating sources. Group claims by a reasoned conceptual argument and explain convergences, disagreements and omissions. Bibliographic breadth, a readable narrative and a reproducible search answer different quality questions; do not treat them as interchangeable.

## Researcher decision checks

Record coverage boundaries, search and selection claims actually executed, conceptual organization, comparison criteria, potential counterexamples and a distinction between the author's own contribution and surveyed work.

## Limits and counterexamples

Ten rules are editorial writing guidance, not a reporting standard for a systematic review. A compelling argument is not evidence that every relevant source was retrieved.
