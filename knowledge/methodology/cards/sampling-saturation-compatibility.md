# Sampling adequacy must match the qualitative analytic tradition

Card ID: sampling-saturation-compatibility
Publication state: DRAFT_NEEDS_INDEPENDENT_EDITORIAL_AND_RIGHTS_REVIEW
Research evidence grade: [I] ORIGINAL_EDITORIAL_SYNTHESIS_ONLY

## Methodological question

When a reviewer asks for a fixed sample-size threshold or a claim of saturation in a qualitative study.

## Original conceptual guidance

Different analytic traditions define adequacy differently. Operational theme-count saturation suits some inductive thematic analysis designs; a reflexive approach emphasizes interpretive meaning and reflexivity; grounded theory uses theoretical development and sampling decisions. Explain which criterion matches the research question.

## Researcher decision checks

State tradition, case heterogeneity, data adequacy logic, stopping or revision rule and why quantitative issues or agreement metrics do or do not fit.

## Limits and counterexamples

From not transfer to number of interviews, saturation ratio or code agreement threshold from one methodological paradigm to all others. Avoid post-hoc certainy unsupported by records.
