## Methodological decision

How does presentation quality help readers inspect evidence?

## Independent editorial guidance

Use readable labels, units, captions and consistent notation, linking each graphic to its exact analytical purpose. Separate design improvement from changes to results.

## What not to infer

Polished figures can still hide missing data, invalid assumptions or misleading uncertainty intervals.
