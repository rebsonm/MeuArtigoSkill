## Intended methodological question

What threatens a causal conclusion from before/after comparisons across treated and comparison groups?

## Original editorial guidance

Define treated units, treatment timing, comparison groups, outcome window and causal estimand. Explicitly justify plausible counterfactual trends. For adoption in different periods, inspect heterogeneity and weights before trusting a conventional two-way fixed-effects coefficient.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Check pre-period measurements, plausible anticipation, alternative group trends, clustering, sensitivity to parallel-trend deviations, selection and timing effects.

## Limitations and counterevidence

A non-significant pretrend test is not proof of parallel trends. Method selection depends on treatment timing and identified estimands, not only software availability.
