## Intended methodological question

How can a document-based study explain why certain sources were selected and how they support its claims?

## Original editorial guidance

Build a source-selection framework before interpretation. Classify each record's authority, origin, intended audience, time and likely omissions. Make analytic units and comparison criteria transparent so interpretations are not conflated with what a document literally states.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Preserve document-level provenance, coding decisions, exclusion reasons, discrepancies across records and contextual notes about the production of each source.

## Limitations and counterevidence

A document can be authentic yet inaccurate, incomplete or strategic. Unrestricted online access does not establish public redistributability.
