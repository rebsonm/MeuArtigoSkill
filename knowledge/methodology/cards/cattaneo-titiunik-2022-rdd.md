## Intended methodological question

Under which institutional assignment rules can differences just across a cutoff identify a local effect?

## Original editorial guidance

Document the running variable, assignment threshold, treatment receipt and local causal estimand. Justify why units immediately above and below a cutoff are comparable and whether manipulation or anticipatory responses could invalidate the design.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Record sorting tests, covariate balance, local bandwidth choice, polynomial specification, bias correction, robustness and treatment compliance.

## Limitations and counterevidence

RDD inference is usually local to the cutoff. This reviewed publication is copyrighted; publish only independent editorial interpretation without reproducing its figures or tables.
