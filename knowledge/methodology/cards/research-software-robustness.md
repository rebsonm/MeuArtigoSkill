## Methodological decision

What must be tested before software output becomes part of scientific evidence?

## Independent editorial guidance

Separate unit, integration and negative-case tests from external or construct validation. Keep dependency versions, synthetic fixtures and diagnostics auditable.

## What not to infer

A passing CI cannot prove that a scientific inference is correct or that real data were collected appropriately.
