## Methodological decision

What safeguards apply when a language model assists design, analysis or drafting?

## Independent editorial guidance

Record model identity, actual prompts, input permissions, citations checked and human adjudication. Treat generated analyses as candidate interpretations unless grounded in executed procedures and source records.

## What not to infer

Fluent model text cannot substitute for scientific execution, source verification or ethical authorization.
