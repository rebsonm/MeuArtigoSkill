## Cut information at source — subject to conference by the researcher

The article argues that incorporating a chatbot into the qualitative process extends the obligations of reflexivity around biases, prompt decisions, systems and interpretive responsibility.

## Aplicação metodológica proposta [I]

For a study using LLM, create a decision-making record on model choice, document selection, human role and consent limits; this is a proposal for governance of analysis, not a scientifically validated procedure.

## Three questions for scientific decision [P]

- What decisions of analysis were effectively human?
- How can model choices and instructions shift meanings?
- Há restrição ética sobre conteúdo enviado ao modelo?

## Contestação e limites
