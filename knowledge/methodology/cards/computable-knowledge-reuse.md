## Methodological decision

What lets another researcher reuse an executable methodological rule responsibly?

## Independent editorial guidance

Publish machine-readable inputs, outputs, assumptions, licenses, controlled vocabularies and version history. Keep private records separate and document boundary conditions for reuse.

## What not to infer

A machine-readable knowledge object can be technically reusable but scientifically inappropriate in a new setting.
