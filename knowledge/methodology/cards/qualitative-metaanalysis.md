# Qualitative meta-analysis requires interpretive synthesis logic

## Methodological question

When a project aims to synthesize qualitative studies without pretending to pool commensurable numeric effect sizes.

## Original conceptual guidance

Clarify the type of product: interpretive reconstruction, meta-synthesis, thematic aggregation or quantitative meta-analysis. The same label can hide incompatible assumptions about units of analysis, context and inferential generalization.

## Researcher decision checks

Define which texts, meanings, findings and conceptual levels are comparable, and why that comparison is defensible. Trace each synthetic claim to the included study and document interpretive transformations.

## Limits and counterexamples

Do not apply numerical meta-analysis formulas to qualitative findings by analysis or assumes all qualitative syntheses share one epistemological model. Check the quoted paper before attributing a precise definition.
