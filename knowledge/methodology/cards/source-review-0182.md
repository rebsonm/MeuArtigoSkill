## Authorial guidance for research

It advocates disclosure of data, methods and institutional incentives to transparency. For responsible use, verify the integral work, place the method in the object, examine alternatives and recover the evidence used in the argumentation.

## Contraprova e limitação

Exemplar came from ResearchGate with Science content; there is no individual license for republishment. Also ask if another interpretation of the same material or a rival methodological strategy is compatible with the data. An editorial or didactic position is not experimental superiority test.
