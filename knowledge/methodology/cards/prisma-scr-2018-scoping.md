## Questão metodológica

The question is to map concepts, types of evidence and gaps, rather than estimate comparable effects?

## Authorial summary for decision

Adopt the appropriate reporting extension to the scope review; declare the justification of mapping, universe of sources, eligibility rules, selection, data charting and descriptive synthesis. Keep explicit the difference between cataloguing the field and critically evaluating effectiveness.

## Conditions of implementation and audit

Mapping question, types of sources, bases consulted, selection process, extracted variables, results by category and gaps.

## Alertas e limites

PRISMA-ScR does not convert an exploratory review into a meta-analysis and does not require all scope reviews to assess risk of bias as in effectiveness reviews.
