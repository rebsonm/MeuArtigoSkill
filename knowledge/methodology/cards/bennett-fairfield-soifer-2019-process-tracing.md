## Intended methodological question

What distinguishes testing a mechanism within cases from merely listing events chronologically?

## Original editorial guidance

Specify the proposed causal mechanism, sequence of intermediate observations, competing explanations and which evidence would discriminate them. The inferential strength of a document or interview depends on its expected occurrence under rival hypotheses.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Map each hypothesized mechanism link to expected observable implications and record evidence for and against the explanation, including selective survival of records.
