## Intended methodological question

What distinguishes a systematic attempt to synthesize evidence from a literature review with selective sources?

## Original editorial guidance

Make the question and admissible study types explicit, document search coverage, study-selection logic, appraisal and transparent synthesis. Separate what was retrieved from what was actually included and why.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Record database and date, exact search strings, duplicate handling, inclusion and exclusion reasons, study quality and evidence limitations.
