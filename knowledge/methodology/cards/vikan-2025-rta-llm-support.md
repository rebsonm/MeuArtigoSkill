## Intended methodological question

How should AI suggestions be compared with human analytic decisions in reflexive thematic analysis?

## Original editorial guidance

Define in advance the analytical role of AI: prompts, examples, alternative readings or organization of data. Log suggestions separately from the investigator's own reflexive interpretation and compare disagreements rather than hiding them.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Preserve model/prompt version, confidentiality conditions, human decision logs, source quotations where permitted and negative or unexpected interpretations.

## Limitations and counterevidence

Claims about efficiency or quality require a design that measures those outcomes; a demonstration of AI use is not independent evidence of improved scientific validity.
