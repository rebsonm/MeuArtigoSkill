## Intended methodological question

When can a configuration of conditions offer a better explanation than independent linear effects?

## Original editorial guidance

Define outcome and condition sets, justify calibration thresholds and evaluate necessary and sufficient configurations. Check logical remainders and contradictory configurations before producing a solution.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Record case selection, thresholds, consistency and coverage, counterexamples, sensitivity to calibration and theoretical expectations for asymmetry.

## Limitations and counterevidence

A minimized Boolean expression is not automatically a causal mechanism. Configurational fit is sensitive to calibration and case composition.
