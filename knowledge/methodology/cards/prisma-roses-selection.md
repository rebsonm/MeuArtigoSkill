# Reporting review designs: do not force one checklist everywhere

## Methodological question

When selecting reporting guidance for different evidence synthesis products.

## Original conceptual guidance

A reporting guideline should represent the actual synthesis design and domain. PRISMA 2020 was designed for systematic review reports, whereas ROSES explicitly addresses environmental systematic evidence reviews and maps. The choice follows the review output and reporting needs, not the most recognizable acronym.

## Researcher decision checks

Record the review question, protocol policy, screening route, appraisal approach and expected result. State the applicable primary guideline and justify the use of domain extensions or crosswalks.

## Limits and counterexamples

Do not mistake a reporting checklist for a recipe for scientific rigor. Never imply that one standard is mandatory for all interdisciplinary management studies.
