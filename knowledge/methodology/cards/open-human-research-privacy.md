## Methodological decision

Which parts of a research project can be made open when people are involved?

## Independent editorial guidance

Separate code, de-identified aggregates, restricted interviews and licensed sources. Explain consent boundaries and use controlled access where full public release is inappropriate.

## What not to infer

Publicly accessible material may still entail privacy risks or lack redistribution permission.
