## Intended methodological question

What must a researcher document when developing interpretive themes rather than reporting pre-existing categories?

## Original editorial guidance

Treat themes as researcher-constructed patterns of shared meaning. Maintain an evolving reflexive account of coding, provisional theme boundaries, alternative readings and the eventual conceptual claim. Describe assumptions about knowledge and interpretation.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Record analytic memos, changes in candidate themes, interpretive choices, excluded material and fit between theme names and evidence.

## Limitations and counterevidence

Agreement between coders is not automatically an appropriate quality criterion for a reflexive approach. Theoretical positioning cannot be delegated to a software tool.
