## Methodological decision

Which methodological evidence should be preserved outside the main manuscript?

## Independent editorial guidance

Use supplements to document transformation rules, full specifications, sensitivity analyses, code and traceable links to claims in the paper. Organize versions so readers can locate the exact supporting artifact.

## What not to infer

Supplementary files must not contain unlicensed third-party originals or confidential sources just to make a paper appear reproducible.
