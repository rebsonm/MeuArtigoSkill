## Methodological question

How can different literature-review designs be compared without imposing the same appraisal standards on every type?

## Original editorial guidance for decision-making

Compare the four SALSA operations: Search, AppraisaL, Synthesis and Analysis. First establish the purpose of the review type, then determine whether exhaustive retrieval, formal appraisal or theoretical integration is appropriate. A scoping review may map a field and its gaps, while an integrative synthesis may prioritize conceptual reconstruction.

## Application and audit conditions

For each viable alternative, state what will be searched, how sources will be assessed, which form of synthesis is appropriate and what inference the analysis can support.

## Warnings and limitations

SALSA is a comparison framework, not proof that an executed review is rigorous. The original article is rights-protected; publish this independent guidance rather than reproducing its protected tables.
