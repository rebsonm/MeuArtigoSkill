# PRISMA 2020: transparent reporting, not a conduct certificate

Card ID: prisma-2020-reporting
Publication state: DRAFT_NEEDS_INDEPENDENT_EDITORIAL_AND_RIGHTS_REVIEW
Research evidence grade: [I] ORIGINAL_EDITORIAL_SYNTHESIS_ONLY

## Methodological question

When reporting a completed systematic review or planning what procedural evidence must be retained.

## Original conceptual guidance

Treat reporting guidance as a transparency obligation, not as a methodological design or quality assessment tool. Keep a reproducible record of information sources, complete search strategies, screening exclusions, synthesis decisions, limitations and amendments. A filled flow diagram is not proof of valid eligibility decisions.

## Researcher decision checks

Identify the actual review type and current reporting extension; verify which PRISMA items apply. Preserve retrieval counts and the exact reasons for full-text exclusions, not inferred aggregates. Separate what was planned, what was executed and what the manuscript reports.

## Limits and counterexamples

Do not describe PRISMA compliance as a substitute for protocol, bias appraisal, complete literature search or human scientific review. Do not require PRISMA 2020 for designs where another reporting framework is better suited.
