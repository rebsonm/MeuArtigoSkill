## Questão metodológica

How to present with transparency what was sought, eliminated, examined and synthesized in a systematic review?

## Authorial summary for decision

Use the 27 items of PRISMA 2020 to verify title reporting of data availability, and the flowchart to reconcile identified records, removed before screening, examined, excluded for included reasons and studies. Save the count by origin and distinguish records, reports and studies.

## Conditions of implementation and audit

Conferir checklist oficial, datas, strings, deduplicação, triagem, motivos de exclusão, avaliação de risco de viés e materiais disponíveis; registrar local de resposta a cada item.

## Alertas e limites

PRISMA 2020 is a Directive of REPORT, not a method of revision, seal of conformity or authorisation to invent exclusions. Do not blindly impose effect review checklist on semi-systematic revisions.
