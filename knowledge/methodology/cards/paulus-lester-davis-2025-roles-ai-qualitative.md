## What the source presents (requires source conference to quote)

It investigates how academic texts linguistically build the role of AI in qualitative analysis and which agency displacements result from descriptions.

## Proposition for Skill use [I]

Skill should differentiate propaganda, use report, normative recommendation and empirical demonstration, refusing equivalence between co-researcher metaphors and scientific authorship.

## Scientific decision issues [P]

- Does the text say that the AI acted as an agent or shows evidence of the effect?
- Quem verificou decisões analíticas?
- What vocabulary masks human responsibilities?
