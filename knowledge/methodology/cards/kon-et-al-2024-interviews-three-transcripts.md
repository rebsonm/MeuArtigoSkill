## What the source presents (requires source conference to quote)

Comparing ChatGPT 3.5/4 themes with human analysis of three clinical care evaluation interviews.

## Proposition for Skill use [I]

An automatic synthesis should maintain mapping for real excerpts and record themes not observed or interpretively fragile.

## Scientific decision issues [P]

- Cada tema automático possui excerto e participante verificável?
- How were the theme matches calculated?
- The design quantifies errors and omissions, not just convergence?

## Contraponto e delimitações

The extremely small sample, ophthalmological domain and model configuration limit transferability; the editorial edition reserves rights.
