## Intended methodological question

Why might the number of information-rich cases needed to encounter relevant codes vary substantially between projects?

## Original editorial guidance

Sampling adequacy depends on variation in the population, rarity of relevant information and how cases are selected. When using a saturation model, state what is counted as new information and what conditions permit that rule to inform stopping.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Identify population heterogeneity, code discovery process, sample-selection logic, rare subgroups and the effect of alternative stopping criteria.

## Limitations and counterevidence

Simulation results depends on model assumptions; do not convert stylized saturation curves into a universal interview quota or apply to all interpretive paradigms.
