## Methodological decision

What makes a quantitative estimate interpretable and auditable?

## Independent editorial guidance

Write down the target estimand, sampling assumptions, missing-data treatment, dependence and model-checking plan. Keep the reasoning traceable from variable definition to uncertainty and sensitivity analyses.

## What not to infer

Statistical significance does not prove causal validity, substantive importance or correct measurement.
