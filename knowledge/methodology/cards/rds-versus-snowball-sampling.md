## Methodological decision

What is lost when a chain-referral design is described as statistically representative?

## Independent editorial guidance

Specify the eligible network, selection mechanism, recruiter relationships and assumptions about inclusion. Treat referral dependency as part of the inference rather than only a logistical arrangement.

## What not to infer

An RDS label does not automatically establish random selection, remove network bias or make generalization valid.
