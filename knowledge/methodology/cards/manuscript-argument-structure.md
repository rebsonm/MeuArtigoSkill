# Scientific article structure: align claims, evidence and reader navigation

## Methodological question

When preparing or restructuring a manuscript and checking whether its sections lead to the intended inference.

## Original conceptual guidance

Structure is part of the reasoning: connect the research question to the specific claims each section can support. Give each paragraph a distinct job, make assumptions visible and avoid presenting an attractive narrative as a substitute for data or analysis. The hierarchy of sections should expose the chain from design to evidence to discussion.

## Researcher decision checks

Test whether the abstract represents the completed work, claims match actual results, headings correspond to substantive statements, limitations temper conclusions and the reader can locate each evidential support.

## Limits and counterexamples

Writing guidance cannot validate statistical methods, causal identification, sampling, or the accuracy of any substantive claim. The article has a published correction; check the journal's correction notice before attributing detailed original wording.
