## Delimitation of the examined work

It examines documentary analysis planning in qualitative research, with attention to the characteristics of pre-existing documents and the limitations of their selection.

## Aplicação sugerida pela curadoria [I]

Require selection protocol, origin, credibility, documentary unit and justification for excluded documents, without assuming neutrality of institutional archives.
