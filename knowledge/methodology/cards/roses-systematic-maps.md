# ROSES: reporting systematic reviews and evidence maps

Card ID: roses-systematic-maps
Publication state: DRAFT_NEEDS_INDEPENDENT_EDITORIAL_AND_RIGHTS_REVIEW
Research evidence grade: [I] ORIGINAL_EDITORIAL_SYNTHESIS_ONLY

## Methodological question

When planning or reporting evidence synthesis for conservation, environment, territorial development or policy questions with mapping outputs.

## Original conceptual guidance

The reporting framework should match the synthesis product. A systematic map describes the distribution and gaps of available evidence, which is not equivalent to estimating an intervention effect. ROSES adds environmental evidence review and mapping report structures, including forms, process details and flow representation.

## Researcher decision checks

Clarify whether the outcome is an evidence map, review, qualitative synthesis or quantitative synthesis. Record source coverage, screening levels, appraisal choices and metadata used to characterize the evidence landscape.

## Limits and counterexamples

Avoid presenting ROSES as a universal standard for every management review. Its original focus is environmental evidence; any transfer to Administration needs a reasoned scope decision.
