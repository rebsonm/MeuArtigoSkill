## Intended methodological question

Which practical decisions separate a credible regression discontinuity design from a graph with a break?

## Original editorial guidance

Define whether assignment is sharp or fuzzy and whether the outcome can be modeled smoothly near the cutoff absent treatment. Focus on local comparability, bandwidth and treatment compliance instead of extrapolating to the entire population.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Preserve assignment rule, exposure contrast, bandwidth sensitivity, placebo cutoffs, discontinuities in predetermined covariates and exposure timing.
