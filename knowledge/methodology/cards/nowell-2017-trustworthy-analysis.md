## Intended methodological question

What traceable decisions support a reader's assessment of qualitative analytic trustworthiness?

## Original editorial guidance

Maintain a clear decision trail linking research question, data preparation, analytic memos, construction of patterns and limitations. Distinguish the credibility of an interpretation from the procedural consistency of recordkeeping.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Describe transparent documentation of coding choices, reflexivity, alternative explanations and contextual detail sufficient for evaluating transferability.

## Limitations and counterevidence

A complete audit trail does not guarantee the interpretation is correct. The original article has a CC BY-NC license; do not treat its PDF as eligible for unrestricted CC BY publication.
