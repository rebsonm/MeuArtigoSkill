# Case study: specify the case and philosophical orientation

## Methodological question

When a bounded organization, program, process or event must be studied in depth within its context.

## Original conceptual guidance

The object of inquiry is not defined merely by collecting data in one organization. Delimit the case, period, unit of analysis and intended inference. Different case-study traditions bring distinct commitments about propositions, interpretation, comparison and how explanations are warranted.

## Researcher decision checks

State the bounded system, rationale for case selection, data sources, treatment of rival explanations and the intended form of generalization or transferability. Explain whether the inquiry is exploratory, descriptive, explanatory or evaluative.

## Limits and counterexamples

One organization does not automatically mean one case; a case study is not automatically qualitative; triangulation is not a guarantee of truth. Avoid conflating the object, units and data collection methods.
