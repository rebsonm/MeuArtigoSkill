## Cut information at source — subject to conference by the researcher

The authors question the correspondence between statistical text generation by models and meaning interpretation required by qualitative approaches.

## Aplicação metodológica proposta [I]

Before proposing AI in qualitative analysis, delimiting the interpretative tradition and explaining which cognitive acts are assisted, which remain with the researcher and how they would be validated.

## Three questions for scientific decision [P]

- A técnica escolhida demanda atribuição humana de sentido?
- Is the result of the model a hypothesis of reading or based analysis?
- What independent evidence would sustain interpretation?

## Contestação e limites
