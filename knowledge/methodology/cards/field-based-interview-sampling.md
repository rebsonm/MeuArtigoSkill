## Methodological decision

How do recruitment decisions constrain inferences from interviews?

## Independent editorial guidance

Define the intended population or experience, site access, case variation, refusals, and the actual route by which individuals entered the study. Keep an auditable sampling log rather than equating a large sample with contextual representativeness.

## What not to infer

High interview counts cannot remedy inaccessible subgroups, poor questions or systematic selection effects.
