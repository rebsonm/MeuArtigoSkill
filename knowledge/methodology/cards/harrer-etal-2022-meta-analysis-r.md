## Intended methodological question

Which research-design decisions must precede computational synthesis of quantitative effects?

## Original editorial guidance

Align the estimand, measure of effect, eligible evidence and dependence structure before fitting fixed or random effects models. Document extraction, transformation and sensitivity decisions; store code and inputs in authorized research files.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Check comparability of studies, overlapping samples, heterogeneity, effect conversion, missing results, risk of bias and robustness of pooled interpretations.
