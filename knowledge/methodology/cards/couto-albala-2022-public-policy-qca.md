## Intended methodological question

How should a policy evaluation treat conjunctions of institutional conditions rather than assuming one factor explains every outcome?

## Original editorial guidance

Construct theoretically meaningful policy cases, identify causal conditions and define membership in relevant sets. Study multiple viable pathways to an outcome and whether the absence of the outcome follows different configurations.

## Decision checks for the researcher

Justify administrative case boundaries, institutional comparability, choice of calibration anchors, residual ambiguity, contradictory cases and robustness.
